# Super Collaboration Decision Process

## Overview

Suggest a decision process that matches the stakes and amount of disagreement without over-facilitating.

## The Process

### Step 1: Choose the Lightest Suitable Process
Choose one:

- **Consensus check:** Best when disagreement seems small.
- **Pros/cons:** Best when options are known but tradeoffs are unclear.
- **Decision criteria:** Best when participants optimize for different goals.
- **DRI/owner decision:** Best when speed matters or one person owns the outcome.
- **Vote:** Best for low-stakes preference choices.
- **Escalation:** Best when authority, safety, or policy is unclear.

### Step 2: Explain Why It Fits
Give one short reason tied to the conversation state.

### Step 3: Provide Options or Criteria
List the options or criteria the group should decide against.

### Step 4: Name One Next Action
End with the next single action.

## Rules

- Do not force a decision if required facts are missing.
- Do not ask for a vote when there is a clear DRI/owner.
- Do not present your recommendation as group consensus.
- If asked for a recommendation, distinguish “my recommendation” from “the group's decision.”

## Output Shape

```text
Recommended process: <process>
Why: <short reason>
Options / criteria:
- <option or criterion>
- <option or criterion>
Next move: <one concrete action>
```
