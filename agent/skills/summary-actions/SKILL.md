# Super Collaboration Summary Actions

## Overview

Produce concise decision records and next actions that preserve uncertainty and ownership.

## The Process

### Step 1: Determine Whether There Is a Decision
Use “not decided yet” when convergence has not happened.

### Step 2: Capture Rationale
State the main reason, decision criterion, or tradeoff that explains the state.

### Step 3: Preserve Open Risks
Keep remaining concerns or unknowns visible.

### Step 4: List Owners and Next Actions
Do not assign an owner unless the conversation names one or the user asks you to propose owners.

## Decision summary

```text
Decision: <what the group chose, or “not decided yet”>
Rationale: <main reason or decision criterion>
Open risks: <remaining concerns or unknowns>
Owners / next actions:
- <owner>: <action>
```

## Discussion Summary Shape

```text
Topic: <active question or decision>
Where things stand: <one-sentence summary>
Agreed:
- <agreement>
Still open:
- <question/disagreement>
Next actions:
- <owner or “unassigned”>: <action>
```

## Rules

- Keep action items concrete and verifiable.
- Preserve open risks instead of hiding them to make the summary look cleaner.
- If there are no action items, say “No explicit next actions yet.”
