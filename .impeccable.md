## Design Context

### Users
Kordi is for mixed users: solo developers, agent power users, and small teams collaborating through local and bridge-backed conversations.

The desktop product should behave like a communication app first — closer to WeChat or WhatsApp than a generic admin dashboard. Contacts, sessions, projects, and bridge peers should feel like parts of one communication system, not separate enterprise tools.

Users need to:
- chat quickly with local agents and people
- browse contacts and conversations naturally
- switch between sessions without UI jumps or visual instability
- collaborate across projects without losing clarity
- work in both light and dark environments equally well

### Brand Personality
- Fast
- Technical
- Clean and dense

Emotional goals:
- confidence
- precision
- immediacy
- low-friction collaboration

The interface should feel efficient and highly intentional, not decorative for its own sake.

### Aesthetic Direction
Primary reference model: communication app UX (WeChat / WhatsApp style mental model for contacts and conversations), adapted for technical users and agent workflows.

Desired qualities:
- dense but readable
- stable alignment
- crisp hierarchy
- low-noise surfaces
- equal-quality light and dark themes

Anti-references:
- not toy-like
- not visually massive / bloated
- not unaligned
- not dashboard-card spam
- not flashy AI/neon aesthetics

### Design Principles
1. Communication-first structure: chats, contacts, projects, and bridge peers should feel like one coherent messaging environment.
2. Dense without clutter: compact layouts are good, but alignment and rhythm must stay precise.
3. Stability over surprise: selection, widths, timestamps, and panel geometry should not jump when state changes.
4. Technical polish: typography, spacing, and metadata should feel precise and useful, not ornamental.
5. Equal-support theming: light and dark modes should both feel first-class, not primary plus fallback.
